Changelog

What has changed, newest first. Ideas for what comes next are welcome through the feedback panel.

  • 5 September 2026 Feedback panel. Send ideas, bug reports or anything else from inside the timer, with an optional email for a reply. Press F to open it.
  • 5 September 2026 Install it, use it offline. The timer can be installed as an app from Chrome, Edge or a phone's home screen, and opens without internet.
  • 5 September 2026 Stats and streaks. Pomodoros per day for the last week, current and best streak, lifetime totals, and a CSV export. Open them from the bar-chart icon.
  • 5 September 2026 Tasks. Write what you are working on, estimate the pomodoros it needs, and watch the count fill in. The list shows a rough finish time.
  • 5 September 2026 Preset links. Addresses such as /?focus=50&short=10&long=20 set the intervals. Fonts now load from the site itself, so the only third-party request is analytics.
  • 5 September 2026 Safer switching. Changing the session type while a session runs now asks first instead of discarding it.
  • 4 September 2026 First release. The dial, focus and break sessions, minute-by-minute adjustment, a chime, an optional tick, keyboard shortcuts, light and dark themes.
